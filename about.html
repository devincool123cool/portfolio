About Me

Who I Am

I'm Devin Ostrowski, an engineering and automotive technology student from Arlington, Virginia. I'm passionate about solving problems, building things, and working collaboratively with others to create meaningful impact.

My Journey

I spent my freshman year of high school at QSI International School in Djibouti, where I developed cultural awareness and resilience in a multicultural environment. Since returning to the United States, I've been actively involved in my community through athletics, student leadership, and technical pursuits.

What Drives Me

  • Problem Solving: Whether it's optimizing stock portfolios, designing 3D models, or troubleshooting automotive repairs, I enjoy tackling challenges methodically and finding innovative solutions.
  • Technical Excellence: I'm committed to mastering hands-on skills like welding, painting, and design while also pursuing higher education in physics and engineering.
  • Community Impact: I actively contribute to my community through student leadership, volunteer work with UNICEF, and athletic participation.
  • Continuous Learning: I embrace opportunities to grow, whether through formal education, certifications, or real-world work experience.

Cultural Awareness & Adaptability

Living in Djibouti and spending summers in South Africa has given me a high degree of cultural acumen. These experiences have increased my awareness of global perspectives, resilience in unfamiliar situations, and the ability to work effectively in diverse teams.
