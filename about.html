About Me

Who I Am

I'm Devin Ostrowski, an engineering and automotive technology student from Arlington, Virginia. I'm passionate about solving problems, building things, and working collaboratively with diverse teams. My background spans technical skills, leadership experience, and a global perspective shaped by living and studying in multiple countries.

My Journey

I spent my freshman year of high school at QSI International School in Djibouti, where I developed cultural awareness and resilience in a multicultural environment. Since returning to the United States, I've been actively involved in my school community through the Arlington County Student Advisory Board, while also pursuing advanced technical training in automotive collision repair and engineering.

What Drives Me

  • Problem Solving: Whether it's optimizing stock portfolios, designing 3D models, or troubleshooting automotive repairs, I enjoy tackling challenges methodically.
  • Technical Excellence: I'm committed to mastering hands-on skills like welding, painting, and design while also pursuing higher education in physics and engineering.
  • Community Impact: I actively contribute to my community through student leadership, volunteer work with UNICEF, and athletic participation.
  • Continuous Learning: I embrace opportunities to grow, whether through formal education, certifications, or real-world work experience.

Cultural Awareness & Adaptability

Living in Djibouti and spending summers in South Africa has given me a high degree of cultural acumen. These experiences have increased my awareness of global perspectives, resilience in adapting to new environments, and empathy for diverse communities. This foundation shapes how I approach teamwork and problem-solving in any context.
